# Funghi Meteo

Public pages for a microclimate slope-map viewer calibrated for porcini.

The daily map is prepared on a PC and read by the app. Colors are **empirical estimates** — not proof that mushrooms are present, and not a harvest guarantee.

## Public site

- Live: https://rockjoel.github.io/funghi-meteo/
- Contents: home + privacy + account-deletion request
- Languages: Italian and English on the same pages (IT | EN switch)
- Fonts: Fraunces and Figtree (SIL Open Font License 1.1), files and licenses in `fonts/`.

## Notes

Respect local rules, private property, and bans. Do not eat mushrooms without identification by qualified staff.
